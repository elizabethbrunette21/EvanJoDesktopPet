- **Character boundary:** `PetCharacterDefinition.swift` owns generic pose appearances and an optional custom-renderer closure. `EvanJoCharacterDefinition.swift` selects Evan Jo-specific renderers for Idle, Happy, Loading, Thinking, and Sleepy.
- **Composition:** `AppDelegate` explicitly injects `EvanJoCharacterDefinition.character` into `PetWindowController`.
- **Shared engine:** `PetWindowController` is the sole global pose-scheduling owner. One cancellation-safe MainActor Task and generation token replace the former Idle/temporary-pose timers. `PetRootView` still hosts either a supplied custom renderer or the existing static appearance without referencing Evan Jo assets.
- **Automatic behavior:** Auto starts on Idle, waits 6–10 seconds, selects Thinking/Happy/Sleepy with weights 40/35/25, preserves configured durations 5/1.8/8 seconds, returns through Idle, then waits 4–7 seconds. Consecutive expressive repeats are excluded, and Sleepy is excluded for the following two expressive selections.
- **State priority:** Loading override, explicit fixed pose, interaction-triggered Happy, automatic expression, then Idle fallback. Loading is excluded from automatic selection; its existing menu exposure remains unchanged.
- **Interaction/manual behavior:** Click-Happy cancels the prior Auto generation without disabling Auto, completes for 1.8 seconds, returns to Idle, waits the recovery range, and resumes. Repeated interaction clicks are ignored while that sequence is active. Manual selection cancels Auto and restarts the selected renderer; re-enabling Auto clears history and starts a fresh Idle cycle.
- **Idle animation:** The body is stationary and performs occasional automatic double nods only. Its dedicated transparent panel is 160×148 pt; the 123 pt body and 139 pt head frames are unchanged, with an 8 pt resting downshift that preserves the feet's desktop position and adds headroom. A local head-petting gesture uses 2 pt meaningful movement, 7 pt minimum legs, two reversals, 20 pt total travel, a 1.35-second timeout, and a 1.2-second cooldown. It runs the same double nod as automatic Idle, starts six sparkle layers during the second recovery, then restores automatic scheduling. The sparkles scale from 0.42 to 1.18, translate 8.5–13.5 pt outward, hold their final state for 0.26 seconds, fade opacity-only over 0.22 seconds, and reset invisibly. The transparent tracking view resets on exit and button press; a drag also cancels active feedback. `EvanJoIdle` remains the static fallback.
- **Happy animation:** The split head/body character jumps up 8 pt, lands, and rebounds 2 pt once inside a 190×166 transparent panel with an 8 pt resting-content offset for headroom. Seven flower/decorations regions follow distinct two-stage trajectories with 0.04-second staggering, hold, fade without shrinking or returning inward, and reset invisibly. Jump lines emphasize takeoff and fade during landing. `EvanJoHappy` remains the static fallback.
- **Loading animation:** Untouched 1254 × 1254 RGBA body, spinner, and original text sources are preserved under `SourceAssets/Characters/EvanJo/LoadingLayers/`. They are imported as `EvanJoLoadingBody`, `EvanJoLoadingText`, and `EvanJoLoadingSpinner`, except that `EvanJoLoadingText` intentionally uses the separately cleaned runtime PNG in the Asset Catalog; the original source and cleaned runtime variant must not overwrite one another. Loading alone uses a 176×132 pt panel while the body stays centered. One text layer travels linearly from +82 to −180 pt over 2.8 seconds behind the body at its original full opacity and color, leaving only through the clipped left edge. Once fully outside, it waits 0.40 seconds at the left, resets to the right without animation, then waits 0.15 seconds before restarting (0.55 seconds visibly empty). The spinner turns linearly once per 2.8 seconds, and the body moves 0 → −1 → 0 pt over a 1.1-second ease-in-out cycle. `EvanJoLoading` remains the static fallback and Loading remains manual-only.
- **Thinking animation:** Untouched Body/Glasses/Bubble/Dots/Leaf sources and the complete reference remain preserved under `SourceAssets/Characters/EvanJo/`. Thinking uses a 176×154 pt panel, a stable body, a 0.14-second glasses push/0.06-second hold/0.16-second return, bubble at 0.34 seconds, dots at 0.62/0.82/1.02 seconds, and the approved 1.62-second reference-derived leaf path. The leaf holds and fades only at its endpoint; bubble and dots remain visible until the renderer disappears. One cancellable task resets all state on disappearance. Reduce Motion removes glasses translation and leaf travel while retaining semantic fades. `EvanJoThinking` remains the static fallback; Thinking remains a 5-second automatic temporary pose.
- **Sleepy animation:** Original 1024 × 1024 body-back and hand-front RGBA sources remain preserved. Project-owned runtime derivatives match Idle's palette without runtime filters and retain the original alpha/canvas. Sleepy keeps its 176×154 pt panel, 130.7 pt visible character height, scene scale `1.151985`, and 149.26 × 128.53 pt mist. Hand Front is scaled to `0.92` around the finger/glasses contact point for a 31.58 × 43.51 pt visible frame. The runtime stack is mist, body back, a shared normal/reflection glasses group with a clipped local light sweep, hand front, then yellow sparkle. Mist settles at 1.50 seconds; the hand/glasses contact group pushes from 1.20–1.35 and remains raised; reflection crossfades to full white from 1.35–1.47 and remains; a 0.22-second left-to-right sweep is clipped to the glasses region and fades by 1.64; the fixed left-temple sparkle appears at 1.50 and remains. One cancellable task resets without animation on disappearance; Reduce Motion uses a stationary local flash and retains the final white reflection. The signing-disabled Debug build passed; user visual/runtime approval remains pending. `EvanJoSleepy` remains the static fallback and Sleepy remains an 8-second automatic temporary pose.
